GOAD

Family: Real-software emulator  ·  Category: C

Game of Active Directory, an emulated multi-domain AD lab on real Windows Server VMs; strong service, OS, identity, action, and observation fidelity, but absent temporal, defensive, benign, telemetry, and external context.

Best suited for: Credential-based privilege escalation, Network architecture attack-path analysis.
